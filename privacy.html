Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Job Alerts Agent is a personal automation script built and used by a single individual (its author) to manage their own Gmail inbox. It is not distributed, offered to other users, or operated as a public service.

What it accesses

The tool connects to one Gmail account via the Gmail API, using the gmail.modify scope. It only ever reads and moves-to-trash email messages matching two fixed sender addresses: LinkedIn's job alert digests (jobalerts-noreply@linkedin.com) and Indeed's job match emails (donotreply@match.indeed.com). It does not read, search, or act on any other email in the account, and it cannot permanently delete or send email.

What happens to the data

Job listing details (title, company, location, work arrangement, and a link to the listing) are extracted from matching emails and written to a private Notion database owned by the same individual. Processed emails are then moved to Gmail's Trash. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or retained anywhere beyond the author's own Gmail account and private Notion workspace.

Data retention and control

Because this tool has exactly one user — its author — data retention and deletion are entirely under that person's own control via their Gmail and Notion accounts directly.
